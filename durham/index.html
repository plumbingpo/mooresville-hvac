How Durham Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
