Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Raleigh

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
